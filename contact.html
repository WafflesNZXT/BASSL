Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for the league?

You can register as an individual player or as a team by contacting us via email at registration@bassl.org or using the contact form above. We'll guide you through the registration process.

What is the registration fee?

Individual player registration is $50 per season for soccer and $40 for basketball. Team registration is $500 for soccer teams. All fees include insurance, jerseys, and referee costs.

Do I need experience to join?

No! BASSL welcomes players of all skill levels. We focus on community building and fun while maintaining competitive play. Our goal is to provide opportunities for everyone to participate.

When do seasons start?

Soccer season is currently active and runs through October 2024. Basketball season starts September 5, 2024. Registration is ongoing for both sports.

Can I volunteer without playing?

Absolutely! We welcome volunteers for various roles including refereeing, event organization, social media, and community outreach. Contact us at volunteer@bassl.org to get involved.
