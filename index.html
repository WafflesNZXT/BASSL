About BASSL

The Bay Area Shia Sports League is dedicated to fostering community spirit, promoting healthy competition, and bringing together the Shia community in the Bay Area through various sports activities.

🏆 Competitive Sports

We organize competitive leagues in soccer, basketball, and other sports, providing opportunities for players of all skill levels to participate and excel.

🤝 Community Building

Our league serves as a platform for community members to connect, build friendships, and strengthen bonds within the Shia community.

⚽ Multiple Sports

From soccer to basketball, we offer various sports to cater to different interests and provide year-round engagement for our community.
